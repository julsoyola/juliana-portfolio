Dear Google Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Software Engineer, Early Career, Campus position at
Google. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Florida State University
and most recently worked as a Software Engineer at Amazon Prime Video, where
I built backend services, distributed data-processing systems, and AWS
infrastructure for a large-scale metadata platform.

At Prime Video, I worked on the Unified Metadata Platform, which was built to
replace fragmented catalog systems with centralized services managing millions
of content records. I developed Java backend services, designed ingestion
pipelines for partner-supplied metadata, and built the supporting AWS
infrastructure. My work regularly crossed application and infrastructure
boundaries, particularly when unusual production inputs exposed failure cases
that were difficult to reproduce in development.

One project I am especially proud of started during my Prime Video internship.
I designed and built a metadata ingestion service that automated validation
and transformation for malformed partner submissions. Before that service,
some submissions required roughly six hours of manual preprocessing. I owned
the project from technical design through implementation, testing, deployment,
and production rollout. I later returned to the same organization full-time
and continued working on the larger platform and its distributed processing
systems.

My experience has also taken me outside backend development. At the University
of Central Florida's VR Lab, I built interactive applications using Unity,
Unreal Engine, C\#, and VR tooling. At Green Bank Observatory, I worked on
APIs and data systems supporting the Skynet Robotic Telescope Network. More
recently, I have incorporated AI-assisted development tools into my daily
workflow to explore codebases, debug problems, iterate on implementations,
and reduce repetitive development work.

What appeals to me about this role is the breadth. I do not want to limit
myself to one narrow layer of software engineering this early in my career.
I have developed a strong foundation in backend and distributed systems, but
I also enjoy learning unfamiliar technologies and moving across a system when
the problem requires it. Google's ability to let engineers grow across
products, systems, and technical areas is a strong match for how I want to
develop as an engineer.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to
bring the production experience I have gained so far to Google while continuing
to grow across large-scale systems and product engineering.
